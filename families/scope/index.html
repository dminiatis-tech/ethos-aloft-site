    Scope

    A voluntary standard of service

    The Air Carrier Access Act, the law that protects disabled passengers on the airlines, was written for commercial flights, and it rarely reaches private aviation. So for a private trip, supporting a neurodivergent traveler is almost always a choice, not a legal requirement. Ethos Aloft is not a legal-compliance service. Our standard is voluntary: no rule requires it, we believe every traveler deserves it.

    Ethos Aloft provides cabin support and crew training — not clinical, counseling, or behavior-analytic services.
